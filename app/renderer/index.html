Picture guide · embedding

  • 5–20 pictures that all share the thing you're teaching (the same cat, the same art style), with variety in everything else: background, angle, lighting.
  • At least 512 px on the short side; they're centre-cropped to a square.
  • The starting word is a hint of what it is (cat, woman, painting); it makes training faster and steadier.
  • An embedding can only steer what the model already knows how to draw: for a new face or a very specific look, a LoRA learns more.
  • When it's done, type its name in a prompt (for example a portrait of my_cat) or click it in the list above the negative prompt.

Tips for Macs with 8 GB

  • SD 1.5 is the quickest; keep it at Small (512). For ERNIE use Medium (768) and the ⚡ fast engine.
  • Fewer steps = faster: 20–25 for most samplers, 6 in Fast mode. The step box shows the recommended number for each sampler.
  • One big job at a time: avoid generating while an enhance or training is running. Quit other heavy apps.
  • If the Mac feels stuck, use ■ Stop; it stops within a step or two and keeps your other queued jobs.

About

Protoart Studio is a rebuild of the ideas behind DiffusionBee, made for older and smaller Macs, with ERNIE-Image, LoRA stacking and training, presets, ControlNet, face restoration (RestoreFormer or CodeFormer), enhancing, a searchable library and live previews.

Built on open-source work: Hugging Face diffusers & transformers, PyTorch, Apple MLX and mflux (fast ERNIE engine), ERNIE-Image (Baidu, Apache 2.0), Stable Diffusion (CreativeML OpenRAIL-M), ControlNet 1.1 (lllyasviel), RestoreFormer (via GFPGAN, Apache 2.0), CodeFormer (S-Lab License 1.0, non-commercial), the Finegrain Image Enhancer and refiners (MIT; its 4x-UltraSharp upscaler is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial), Florence-2 (MIT), WD tagger (Apache 2.0), Depth Anything V2, Electron and Vue. Models you download keep their own licences.

Speed: a model ready in memory
